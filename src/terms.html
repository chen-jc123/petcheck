What PetCheck is

PetCheck is a shared log and reminder tool for household pet care. It records tasks you report, tells you what's done or due, and can remind you about missed tasks.

Not veterinary advice

PetCheck does not provide veterinary, medical or dietary advice. It doesn't diagnose, interpret symptoms, or recommend doses or treatments. If you're worried about your pet's health, contact a veterinarian. In an emergency, contact an emergency veterinary clinic right away.

Reminders are a convenience

PetCheck depends on people logging tasks, on network services and on email delivery. Reminders and alerts may be late, missing or wrong. You remain responsible for your pet's care; don't rely on PetCheck as the only safeguard for time-sensitive needs such as medication.

Your household key

Keep your household key private. Anyone with it can read and add to your household's log. If it's exposed, contact us to have it replaced.

Acceptable use

Don't use PetCheck to store information unrelated to pet care, to attempt to access other households' data, or to disrupt the service.
